\chapter{Bolt Pretension}
\label{chap:pretension}

Pretension introduces an axial adjustment across a planar cut through a solid
mesh. The supported sequence is a linear-static preload, locking the solved
adjustment, and applying subsequent service loads. This formulation does not
include nonlinear bolt/contact behaviour or follower pretension.

\section{Cut and scalar control}

The cut must consist of existing element faces and completely separate two
solid regions. Initially shared interface nodes are duplicated and one side is
rewired before user fields and constraints are constructed. Element interiors
are not cut. Already separated faces must have equal node counts and matching
coordinates. Overlapping sections are unsupported.
The element must provide a nondegenerate boundary face; C3D13 boundary
extraction is currently unavailable.

Each section has an isolated control node with only DOF 1 active. For every
paired interface node,
\[
  (\boldsymbol{u}_B-\boldsymbol{u}_A)\cdot\boldsymbol{n}=q.
\]
The two transverse relative displacements remain zero. The control displacement
is the scalar adjustment \(q\), not a physical translation of a mesh point.
Its coordinates do not affect the equations; no artificial stiffness or mass
is added. Positive scalar force is work-conjugate to positive \(q\).

Native input obtains \(\boldsymbol{n}\) from the first \val{SURFACE\_A} face.
Abaqus-compatible input uses the area-weighted average outward surface normal
unless an explicit global normal is supplied. Face orientations must be
consistent. Positive force acts along this normal on the underlying, rewired
B side and oppositely on side A.

Use \val{NULLSPACE} or \val{ELIMINATION} explicitly. \val{LAGRANGE} is rejected
whenever a pretension section exists, including an unloaded section: its current
multiplier regularization does not enforce the adjustment exactly or establish
constraint consistency. Contradictory supports, equations and prescribed or
locked adjustments are rejected by the supported exact methods. General
constraint feasibility does not establish structural stability.

\keywordpage{PRETENSIONSECTION}

The native section card references one merged cut or two separated faces.
\begin{keywordparameters}
\key{NAME} & required & Unique section name. \\
\key{SURFACE\_A} & required & First element-face set. \\
\key{SURFACE\_B} & optional & Coincident matching second set; defaults to the first set. \\
\key{NODE} & optional & Existing isolated node or singleton NSET; omitted to generate a control node. \\
\end{keywordparameters}

\begin{dialectexamples}
\begin{femasterdeck}
*SURFACE, SFSET=CUT_FACE
2, 2, 1
*PRETENSIONSECTION, NAME=PT1, SURFACE_A=CUT_FACE
\end{femasterdeck}
\begin{noabaqus}
Use \cmd{PRE-TENSION SECTION} with an explicit existing control node.
\end{noabaqus}
\end{dialectexamples}

The native surface row is surface ID, element ID and local face number.
An exterior face or an incomplete separating cut is rejected. For separated
faces, specify \val{SURFACE\_A=CUT\_A, SURFACE\_B=CUT\_B}.

\keywordpage{PRETENSION}

This native loadcase command selects the section control.
\begin{keywordparameters}
\key{SECTION} & required & Previously defined section. \\
\key{ACTION} & required & \val{LOAD} or \val{LOCK}. \\
\key{CONTROL} & for LOAD & \val{FORCE} or \val{DISPLACEMENT}. \\
\key{VALUE} & for LOAD & Total signed section force or relative axial adjustment. \\
\end{keywordparameters}

Force control applies the entire section force once to the scalar DOF; it is
not a force per interface node. Displacement control prescribes \(q\).
An uncontrolled native section remains tied at zero gap.

LOCK requires a successful preceding adjustment and holds that solved value.
It persists into later loadcases; repeating LOCK is harmless. Service loading
changes the transmitted force while the held adjustment remains fixed.

The following sequence assumes a named support collector \val{BC} and external
load collector \val{SERVICE} have already been defined. Select native supports
and external collectors in the applicable loadcases.
\begin{femasterdeck}
*LOADCASE, TYPE=LINEARSTATIC, NAME=PRELOAD
*SUPPORTS
BC
*PRETENSION, SECTION=PT1, ACTION=LOAD, CONTROL=FORCE, VALUE=10000
*CONSTRAINTMETHOD, TYPE=ELIMINATION
*END
*LOADCASE, TYPE=LINEARSTATIC, NAME=HOLD
*SUPPORTS
BC
*PRETENSION, SECTION=PT1, ACTION=LOCK
*CONSTRAINTMETHOD, TYPE=ELIMINATION
*END
*LOADCASE, TYPE=LINEARSTATIC, NAME=SERVICE
*SUPPORTS
BC
*PRETENSION, SECTION=PT1, ACTION=LOCK
*LOADS
SERVICE
*CONSTRAINTMETHOD, TYPE=ELIMINATION
*END
\end{femasterdeck}

Replace the first LOAD control with
\val{CONTROL=DISPLACEMENT, VALUE=0.001} to prescribe the adjustment.
A native CLOAD on a node set applies its force to each node. Native SUPPORT
rows specify component values, not Abaqus first/last DOF ranges.

\keywordpage{PRE-TENSION SECTION}

The Abaqus-compatible section card is supported at root and Assembly scope.
\begin{keywordparameters}
\key{SURFACE} & required & Merged planar element-face set. \\
\key{NODE} & required & Existing isolated control node or singleton NSET. \\
\key{FOLLOWER} & optional & Only \val{NO} is supported. \\
\end{keywordparameters}

One optional data row supplies three finite, nonzero global normal components.
Otherwise the consistent average surface normal determines the axis. A section
inside PART or INSTANCE is unsupported. Root/Assembly cards may reference
compiled Instance surfaces and nodes such as \val{BOLT.CUT} and
\val{BOLT.900001}; assembly singleton aliases are also accepted.

The control node must be declared explicitly. Its DOFs 2--6, structural-element
attachment, shared use by sections and local transformations are rejected.
\begin{abaqusdeck}
*NODE
900001, 0, 0, 0
*PRE-TENSION SECTION, SURFACE=CUT, NODE=900001, FOLLOWER=NO
*STEP, NAME=PRELOAD
*STATIC
1, 1
*CLOAD
900001, 1, 10000
*END STEP
*STEP, NAME=HOLD
*STATIC
1, 1
*BOUNDARY, FIXED
900001, 1, 1
*END STEP
\end{abaqusdeck}

For adjustment control, replace CLOAD with BOUNDARY and
\val{900001, 1, 1, 0.001}. FIXED snapshots the previous successful U1 and accepts
no magnitude or amplitude. This FIXED support is limited to the pretension
control DOF; general structural FIXED is unsupported.

\subsection{History and limits}

CLOAD and BOUNDARY have independent history. FIXED does not remove a carried
force. At the control node the scalar reaction is total section force minus
applied scalar load. \val{OP=MOD} replaces the addressed source/DOF;
\val{CLOAD, OP=NEW} clears concentrated loads without removing FIXED;
\val{BOUNDARY, OP=NEW} clears boundaries, including FIXED. Restate physical
supports that must remain after clearing boundaries. Node references and
singleton aliases of the same control node share one history target.

Before its first control definition, an Abaqus-compatible section remains tied
at zero gap. This differs from Abaqus's initially free control DOF. Removing
control after its first use releases the adjustment; it does not reinstate
the initial tie. Relative STEP TIME amplitudes are evaluated at linear step
end; TOTAL TIME and nonlinear evolution are unsupported.

Native PRETENSION actions and Abaqus node conditions cannot be mixed on the
same section. Beam/truss ELEMENT pretension, FOLLOWER=YES, nonlinear procedures,
inertia relief and scalar-load rebalancing are unsupported.

\section{Results and examples}

\val{PTGAP} reports the axial gap on both interface nodes and on the control
node. \val{PTFORC} reports interface-force vectors; sum one face and project
onto the section axis to obtain its signed resultant. Component 1 at the
control node stores that resultant. \val{TF} stores total signed section force
at control nodes and zero elsewhere. There, \(TF=RF_1+F_\mathrm{applied}\).
RF alone is not the total section force when a scalar load is carried.

Keep supports away from the cut: support and interface reactions cannot be
separated by this nodal force recovery. Generated nodes receive unique result
identifiers. Existing user sets retain their membership; the global all-nodes
region gains the new nodes, and owned boundary faces follow rewired elements.
All pretension fields are available in RES, FRD and FEMR. To select them:
\begin{femasterdeck}
*NODE FILE
U, RF, TF, PTFORC, PTGAP
\end{femasterdeck}

Complete examples are \texttt{095\_bolt\_pretension.inp} and
\texttt{096\_bolt\_pretension\_abaqus.inp} in
\texttt{examples/09\_constraints}. Analytical tests and verification commands
are documented in \texttt{documentation/pretension-validation.md}.
